# Support

Email **[robane.games@gmail.com](mailto:robane.games@gmail.com)**. We read
everything and answer as quickly as a small team can, usually within a few
days.

## What to include

- **Which game** — Word Popping!, Pic Sort, or Cube Out 3D
- **Your phone** — model and operating system version (for example
  "iPhone 13, iOS 18" or "Nokia 3.1 Plus, Android 10")
- **Where it happened** — the level number and what you were doing
- **What you expected and what you saw** — a screenshot or a short screen
  recording helps more than anything else

## Purchases and refunds

Purchases are sold and processed by the store, not by us. For Apple App Store
purchases, request a refund at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>. For Google
Play purchases, use Google Play's refund process. We cannot issue refunds
ourselves; nothing here affects your statutory consumer rights.

## Privacy requests

To exercise any of the rights described in the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/) — access, erasure, objection, withdrawing
consent — email the address above. We hold no name or account for you, so
tell us roughly when you installed the game and the level you had reached, so
that we can find the record keyed to your random player id.
