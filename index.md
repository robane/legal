# robane — support & legal

**robane** publishes small puzzle games for phones. These pages cover every
game we publish:

- **Word Popping!** — a word-association puzzle for iPhone (App Store)
- **Pic Sort** — a picture-sorting puzzle for iPhone (App Store)
- **Cube Out 3D** — a 3D cube-shooting puzzle for Android (Google Play)

## Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with a game? Email us and we will get back
to you:

**[robane.games@gmail.com](mailto:robane.games@gmail.com)**

When reporting a bug, it helps to include the game's name, your phone model,
the operating system version, and the level you were playing. See the
[support page](./support/) for details.

### Common questions

**Is my progress backed up?**
It depends on the game. Word Popping! and Cube Out 3D save your progress on
your device and back it up automatically, so deleting and reinstalling on the
**same device** restores your levels and coins; it does **not** transfer to a
different device, because there is no account to sign in to. Pic Sort keeps
your progress on your device only.

**Why am I seeing ads?**
Our games are free to download and ad-supported. Whether the ads are
personalized is up to you: on iPhone the system asks at first launch whether
the game may track you, and in Europe and the UK every game also shows an
advertising consent dialog. Decline either and you still see ads, just
contextual ones. You can change your mind at any time in the game's
**Settings → Privacy**.

**Can I buy anything?**
Where a game offers purchases (Word Popping! and Pic Sort sell optional coin
packs through the App Store), the store processes the payment and handles
refunds — for Apple at <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>. Cube Out 3D has no
purchases at this time.
